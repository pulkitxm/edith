# Video editor

[Back to the CLI reference](../README.md)

Enable **Video editor** in Edith's Extensions screen under **Media**. The editor then appears in the Media section of the sidebar. It runs in the Edith window and does not need Screen Recording permission.

The editor opens on your saved projects, shown as cards with a still from each project's video; select one to open it. Choose **Import video** to start a new one, or use **Add clips** to add more videos, images, or audio. The left rail lists your clips. Select one to move to it; use the timeline ruler to seek, press Space to play or pause, and use **Split** to cut at the playhead.

Choose **Zoom** above the timeline and add a zoom at the playhead. Edith shows the full video frame with a highlighted rectangle representing the area that will fill the video; drag the rectangle to frame what matters. Pick a zoom amount and length beside the timeline. Zooms appear on their own timeline track: select one to adjust its framing, drag its body to move it, or drag an edge to change its length. **Text** adds a timed callout, and **Transition** offers a cut, fade through black, or flash between adjacent clips. Use **Export** for an MP4 or GIF. Video renders at a steady 60 frames per second, so zooms stay smooth even over parts of a screen recording where nothing moved. After you pick where to save, the export window shows its progress and the time left. **Hide** closes it while the export carries on, and the **Export** button shows the percentage and reopens it. If you close the Edith window or quit during an export, Edith keeps running until the export finishes, then quits and posts a notification if Edith may send them.

Projects save in Edith's local video-project library. When opening an OpenScreen project, Edith saves its own copy before editing. Keep the original media files available; if one moves, reopen the project and locate it when prompted.
