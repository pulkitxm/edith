# Governance

## Project Stewardship

Edith is maintained by [Pulkit](https://github.com/pulkitxm), who is the project
steward and final decision maker. The steward manages releases, repository
permissions, security response, and the project's technical direction.

## Decisions

Routine changes are decided through focused issues and pull requests. Larger
features, compatibility changes, privacy changes, and architectural work should
begin with a public issue so goals and tradeoffs can be discussed before
implementation. Decisions favor user safety, local data ownership, native macOS
behavior, predictable automation, and long-term maintainability.

When consensus is not clear, the steward records the decision and its reasons
in the relevant issue or pull request. Security-sensitive decisions may remain
private until coordinated disclosure is complete.

## Contributions and Maintainers

Anyone may report issues, propose changes, review pull requests, or improve
documentation. Repository permissions may be extended to contributors who
demonstrate sustained, constructive participation, sound judgment, and care for
the project's users. Access can be reduced when a maintainer becomes inactive
or no longer follows the Code of Conduct or security practices.

Contributors retain copyright in their work and provide it under the project's
GNU General Public License v3.0 when their contribution is accepted.
