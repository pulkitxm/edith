Soundboard recordings

All sounds are distributed under CC0 1.0 Universal.
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The source high-quality MP3 previews (Freesound) and original Ogg files
(Kenney and OpenGameArt) were converted to 48 kHz stereo 16-bit PCM WAV.
Levels were adjusted toward -17 dBFS active RMS with peaks capped at -3 dBFS.
Each clip has a 3 ms fade-in and a 25 ms fade-out. Applause and thunder
have 400 ms fade-outs. Short clips use a fade-out of at most 10% of their length.
