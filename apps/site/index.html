FAQ

Questions, answered.

How much does Edith cost? +

Nothing. Edith is free and open source under the GPL-3.0 licence. Every feature is in the one app, with no paid tier, no licence key, and no account. The full source is on GitHub.

Why can I not install Edith without the privacy and security warning? +

Our Apple Developer account approval is still in progress, so builds are not yet notarized by Apple. We are working closely with Apple and this will be resolved soon. Until then, allow the app in System Settings under Privacy & Security by clicking Open Anyway. The video below walks through the exact steps.

Does Edith send my usage data anywhere? +

No. Usage stays on your device, with no account and no telemetry. Optional iCloud backup merges your history across your own Macs.

Which systems does Edith support? +

The full app runs on macOS 14 and later on Apple Silicon. The native Ubuntu preview targets Ubuntu 24.04 LTS on amd64.

Can I contribute? +

Yes. The whole app is on GitHub. Issues and pull requests are welcome, and the README covers building Edith from source.
