Get Edith

Overview

Edith adds a virtual camera you can pick in Zoom, Meet or FaceTime. Frame your shot, change your look or background, play a video or share a window, and step away behind a card. Nothing leaves your Mac.

How it works

  1. 1

    Check your preview

    Allow camera access and pick a camera, a video or a window as your source.

  2. 2

    Frame and style it

    Zoom, pan, change the look or background, and save the result as a scene.

  3. 3

    Choose it in your meeting app

    Select Edith Camera as the camera in Zoom, Meet or FaceTime.

  4. 4

    Step away privately

    Pause behind a card, a blank screen or a frozen frame, then go live again.

From the terminal

Every example returns JSON with --json, and agents can call the same thing over MCP.

$ ed camera status
$ ed camera scene list
$ ed camera scene next
$ ed camera pause --style card
