Color Picker

Pick any color on your screen and copy it in the format you need.

Desk⌃⌥⌘C
$ed extensions install colorPicker
Get Edith

Overview

Press the shortcut to get a magnifier you can move over anything on screen. Click to copy the exact color as hex, RGB, HSL or code. Recent colors are kept so you can copy them again later.

How it works

  1. 1

    Press the shortcut

    A magnifier appears. You can also choose Pick Color in settings.

  2. 2

    Click a color

    Move over any pixel on screen and click to sample it.

  3. 3

    Paste it anywhere

    The color is copied in your chosen format, such as #1A73E8.

  4. 4

    Reuse recent colors

    Click a swatch in settings to copy it again.

From the terminal

Every example returns JSON with --json, and agents can call the same thing over MCP.

$ ed color pick
$ ed color ls --limit 5
$ ed color copy 1 --format rgb
