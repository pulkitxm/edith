Screen Recorder

Record your screen, or speed up a long session into a short time-lapse.

Media
$ed extensions install timeLapse
Get Edith

Overview

Record a display or chosen windows at normal speed, or as a time-lapse that captures a frame every few seconds. Recording keeps running if you leave the page. Export a video when you are done. Everything stays on your Mac.

How it works

  1. 1

    Choose what to record

    Pick a display or specific windows. macOS asks for Screen Recording access the first time.

  2. 2

    Press Record

    Add audio or the cursor under Audio & options, then stop whenever you are done.

  3. 3

    Export a video

    Select a recording, choose a quality and save it as a video file.
