Hinge

Fold your desktop as you close the lid.

LabsLabs⌃⌥H
$ed extensions install hinge
Get Edith

Overview

Hinge bends your desktop like a hinge when you close the laptop lid, using the lid angle sensor. It is a visual effect only: nothing on your Mac changes. Turn it on with the switch or the shortcut, and turn it off the same way.

How it works

  1. 1

    Turn on the fold

    Use the switch in settings or press Control Option H.

  2. 2

    Set your open angle

    Open the lid the way you normally work and press Use current angle so Hinge knows what open means.

  3. 3

    Shape the effect

    Adjust strength, side fill and blur until the fold looks right.

Shortcut

⌃⌥H
HingeTurns the desktop fold on or off
